Position

A building should look like what it is made of, holding up what it holds up, and nothing more.

We don't dress a structure in a facade it doesn't need. Where a wall carries load, you see the wall. Where a joint is welded, you see the weld. Clients come to us when they are tired of buildings that lie about how they stand.

Structure is the drawing

The load path is the plan. We design the frame first and let every surface follow from it, rather than styling a skin over an afterthought of a structure.

Material ages in public

Concrete streaks, steel rusts, timber greys. We choose materials that are allowed to weather, and we detail joints so that weathering looks intended, not neglected.

Cost is a design constraint

A budget is not an obstacle to good work, it's an input. Our best buildings came from tight ones — poured on site, formed with local timber, finished once.
